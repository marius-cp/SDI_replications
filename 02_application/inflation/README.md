## Replication Material for Application: Survey Forecasts of U.S. Annual CPI Inflation

- The folder `murphy_replication` contains the data preparation. Its contents are downloaded from  
  https://sites.google.com/site/fk83research/code, where the replication material of Ehm et al (2016) is provided.  The data sets used here were updated accordingly.

- The file `inflation.R` produces the results shown in the paper.

- `MZdiagnostic.R`  
  Produces the Mincer--Zarnowitz regression diagnostic plots shown in the Appendix.
